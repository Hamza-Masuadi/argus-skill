# Saltline

Build the website for Saltline, a small sea-kayak guiding company. You have complete creative freedom: develop the identity, typography, colour, layout, motion and copy yourself. Do not ask me to choose anything. It should feel like a real, loved local outfit with an exceptionally good website, not a template.

## The business
- Saltline Sea Kayaking, Falmouth, Cornwall, guiding since 2014. Founder and lead guide: Morwenna Pascoe (British Canoeing Advanced Sea Leader). Four guides. Groups of six at most, all kit included, no experience needed for the half-day trips.
- Customers: holidaymakers and locals, 25 to 60, many first-timers who are a little nervous about the sea; a few experienced paddlers after multi-day trips. Most arrive from a search like "sea kayaking Falmouth" or a friend.
- Why Saltline: six people per guide, never more; trips are planned around the day's tide and wind, so the route changes and the guide rings the evening before to confirm it; if the sea says no, you rebook or get a full refund.
- Main action: book a trip (choose a trip, a date and the number of people; deposit on booking, no real payment here). Second: ask a question (a short form).

## Trips (invent detail in the same spirit)
- Helford River half day, 3 hours, £55, flat water, first-timers and children 10+.
- Coves and caves, 4 hours, £70, open coast, some swell, seals most days.
- Sunset paddle, 2.5 hours, £50, May to September.
- Lizard two-day expedition with a beach camp, £260, experience needed.
Each trip needs: who it is for, what you will see, where it starts, what the sea is usually like, what is included, the months it runs.

## Page
One page plus a working booking flow (trip, date, people, details, confirmation; no real payment, do not pretend one was taken). It should include: an opening that makes you want to be on the water, the trips, how a day goes and how the sea decides the route, safety answered honestly, the guides, where to meet, and a strong ending. Many visitors are on a phone, on holiday.

## Photographs
Use real stock photographs of sea kayaking, coast, coves and seals, chosen as one consistent, sharp, well-lit set. The business is invented: say so once in the footer.

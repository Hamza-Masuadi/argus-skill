# Hollin Down

Build the website for Hollin Down, a small English vineyard. You have complete creative freedom: develop the identity, typography, colour, layout, motion and copy yourself. Do not ask me to choose anything. It should feel like a real, well-loved estate with an exceptionally good website, not a template.

## The business
- Hollin Down, an 11-hectare vineyard on chalk above Alfriston, East Sussex. Planted in 2011 by Ruth and Samir Hollin; first vintage 2015. Sparkling wine by the traditional method and one still Chardonnay. About 38,000 bottles a year, all from their own fruit.
- Visitors and buyers: couples and small groups from London and Brighton for a tour and tasting; locals for the cellar door; a wine club of about 900 members. Most arrive from a recommendation, a restaurant list, or a search like "vineyard tour Sussex".
- Why Hollin Down: every bottle from their own vines; tours led by the people who grow and make it, twelve guests at most; the tasting is at one long table overlooking the vines; open all year, with the work of the season shown as it is.
- Main action: book a tour and tasting (date, time, number of guests; no real payment here). Second: join the wine club; third: buy a bottle.

Sunday Larder, letter 61 · 23 November

The week I stopped peeling squash

Roast it in its skin, cut side down, at 200°C until a knife slides in without asking. The skin goes soft and slightly bitter, which is the point; it stands up to brown butter with sage and a little anchovy.

If you only cook one thing from this letter, make the crispy chickpeas. They keep for a week in a jar, if they last that long.

Until next Sunday, Nell

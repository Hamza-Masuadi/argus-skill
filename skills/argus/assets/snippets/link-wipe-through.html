The orchard that waited forty years for its first pear

Ruth Allard planted the Doyenné du Comice row in 1984, on the advice of a grafting manual she later called optimistic. The trees took. The fruit did not, until a cold March in 2024 and a borrowed hive from the Stour Valley beekeepers.

This autumn the row gave eleven crates. Four went to Saturday market, the rest to perry.

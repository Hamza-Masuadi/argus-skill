How a pot is thrown

Every piece from Gull Studio is made start to finish by one potter. Here is the order of work for a single mug.

01 / 04

  1. 1. Wedge

    The clay is kneaded for ten minutes to push out air. Trapped air is what makes pots crack in the kiln.

  2. 2. Throw

    Centred on the wheel, opened with the thumbs and pulled up in three passes to a wall about 6mm thick.

  3. 3. Turn and handle

    A day later it is trimmed leather-hard, and a handle is pulled from a separate coil and joined.

  4. 4. Fire and glaze

    Bisque fired, dipped in our ash glaze, then fired again over fourteen hours. Then it is yours.

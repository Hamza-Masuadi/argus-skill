Every bowl goes through fire.

A two-person pottery in Leith. Scroll to watch one bowl go from wet clay to the kiln.

01 / 03

Thrown wet

Six hundred grams of stoneware, centred and opened on the wheel in under four minutes.

02 / 03

Dried slowly

A week under cloth, turned daily, until it rings when tapped and has lost a tenth of its size.

03 / 03

Fired to 1280 °C

Twelve hours in the kiln, and the grey slip turns the deep green it is named for.

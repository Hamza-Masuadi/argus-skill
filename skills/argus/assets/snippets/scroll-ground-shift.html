4.30am

The ovens are lit in the dark.

Two deck ovens take ninety minutes to reach 260°C. The first dough has been proving since yesterday afternoon.

6.15am

First bake, sourdough and rye.

Forty loaves go in at once. They come out darker than most bakeries dare.

8.00am

The doors open on Mill Lane.

By nine the croissants have usually gone. Order the day before and we will keep yours back.

2.00pm

Sold out, cleaned down, home.

Whatever is left goes to the shelter on Cross Street at closing.

# Stage 4: the first screen, to finished quality

**If `.argus/run.md` exists, read it first**, and again after any compaction or when resuming.

The first screen is built alone, looked at and fixed to its bar before any other section exists. A rough is built from this card, the brief, the kit and its dealt direction, to rough finish, stopping before the bar.

Produces: the first screen at finished quality, its tokens, and the bar's answers in `run.md`.

## The rules, most important first

- **In five seconds a stranger sees what it is, who it is for, and the action.** Everything on the first screen serves that. The floor in `SKILL.md` fails here most often: a band or cut-out crossing the headline or form, a headline over a face, a soft or dark photograph, or the visual that explains the claim dropped for a bigger headline.
- **Proof near the action is the default**: one proof line (the rating and its count, say) beside the action, when the owner gave proof. On a full-bleed picture opening it may sit just below the fold. With no proof given, none is written; the bar never asks you to invent it.
- **Carry the direction out fully, at its own volume.** Minimal: one strong thing given room, the type comfortable. Picture-led: the photograph (or a muted loop) given room, often full-bleed, type on its calm area (`heroes.md`, "Full-bleed openings"). Expressive: the idea committed at scale. Half-measures look unfinished, and so does room with nothing strong in it.
- **About five groups**: the header, the headline with its subhead, the action with one reassurance line, the proof line, and the picture. A booking or order form, or the one card holding it, is one group and may be the action; quotes, counts, repeated ratings and a second link go below. The checker counts each text block more than 14px from the next as its own group, so these five measure up to seven; `hero-crowded` prompts above seven.
- **Something answers the visitor**: every control has hover, press and focus, and, unless the owner chose still, the first screen has its arrival (`motion.md`, "How much"). A live element is an option sized to the direction, never in the way of reading or the action, which is there from the first frame.
- **Type fits the direction** (`type.md`): the headline clearly leads; short, saying the difference or the belief. A headline that is itself a control is marked up as `craft.md` says.
- **Copy before layout.** The headline and subhead pass the offer test; the action's label names what the visitor gets (`convert.md`).

## The bar

Judge it as a separate look: fresh captures at 390, 1440 and 1920 plus the in-between widths, as if someone else made it (`shot.js <url> --widths 390,1440,1920 --between --check`: 768, 820 and 1024 get the first screen and the checker only). Answer each in writing:

1. In five seconds, at 390 and 1440, can a stranger say what it is, who it is for, and what to do? If the owner gave proof, is one line of it near the action?
2. At any width, does anything cover, cut across or collide with text, a form, a price or the action, or does any picture show as an empty box? Is any headline over a face or a blurred area?
3. Is every picture sharp, well lit and clearly this kind of business?
4. Is the action obvious, and does it work? Its minimal target (the form or booking step, unstyled is fine) is built now. A headline pair that fails the offer test is a "no" here.
5. Does it fit this business and its visitors: would the owner and their customers recognise it as theirs?
6. Would you show it to the owner as finished: aligned edges, type set by eye, composed at 1920, any room filled by something strong?
7. The swap test: put another business's name on it. Would it still fit? If yes, the idea is not carried: strengthen it with something only this business has (a price, a place, an hour), not with props.

A "yes" to the second or seventh, or a "no" to any other, means rework and a fresh look. Two rounds of rework. Still failing: show the owner the best version and the failed questions, and ask whether to keep going or switch to another direction from the choosing page; wait for the answer. (If the owner said not to ask, or the run is unattended, switch once and say so.) A reworked or swapped composition is never side-by-side unless the owner asked for one or a form must sit beside the headline (`directions.md`, "Dealing"). The bar comes before the loop and is not one of its rounds.

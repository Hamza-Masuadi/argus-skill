# Clockwise

Build the marketing website for Clockwise, a staff rota app for independent cafés and restaurants. You have complete creative freedom: develop the identity, typography, colour, layout, motion and copy yourself. Do not ask me to choose anything. It should feel like a real, well-loved small software company with an exceptionally good website, not a SaaS template.

## The product
- Clockwise: the week's rota built in ten minutes, sent to everyone's phone, with shift swaps the staff sort out between themselves and the manager approves in one tap. Made in Leeds since 2021 by a team of nine; founder Tobi Adeyemi ran a café for six years.
- Customers: owners and managers of independent cafés, restaurants and bars with 5 to 40 staff, who currently use a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp group and a photo of a whiteboard. They are not technical and are usually reading on a phone after closing.
- Why Clockwise: it knows who can work when (availability, contracted hours, who is trained on the bar or the pass), so it drafts a rota you only need to correct; swaps happen without the manager being the go-between; it shows the wage cost of the week against sales before you publish.
- Main action: start a free 30-day trial (email only, no card). Second: see a live example rota you can play with.
- Pricing: £2.50 per staff member a month, first 5 staff free forever, no contract.

## Proof
- 2,300 venues; 41,000 staff on it; Trustpilot 4.8 from 1,270 reviews; average 3.5 hours a week saved on rotas (customer survey, 2026).
- Reviews, word for word: "Sunday night used to be rota night. Now it's ten minutes on the bus." (Ana, café owner, Bristol); "Swaps just happen. I get a tap to approve and that's it." (Callum, bar manager, Glasgow); "Seeing wage cost before I publish has saved me more than the app costs." (Mei, restaurant owner, Manchester).
- What stops people: staff won't download another app; moving off the spreadsheet is a chore; "we're too small"; fear of being locked in.

## Page
One page. It should include: an opening that shows the product doing its job, how a week goes from draft to published to swapped, what staff see on their phones, the wage-cost view, pricing with a simple calculator (number of staff to monthly price), answers to the objections, and a strong ending. The example rota should be real enough to poke: move a shift, see the cost change, approve a swap.

## Pictures
The product itself, built in code with believable data (names, shifts, roles, hours, costs), is the main picture. Photographs of real cafés and staff may be used if they are sharp and well lit. The business is invented: say so once in the footer.

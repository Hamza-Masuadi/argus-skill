01 / 07 Why we print slowly

Seven things we learned printing 4,000 books by hand

Fifteen years at the Albion press, written down for the apprentices.

Damp the paper the night before

Dry paper takes ink badly. We wrap each ream in damp blotters and leave it overnight under a board.

Ink less than you think

Most bad impressions are too much ink, not too little. Roll the slab until it hisses, not until it shines.

Lock up square

A forme that is a degree off square will walk across the sheet over a run of two hundred.

Make ready takes longer than printing

Packing under the low letters is where the evening goes. Budget for it.

Wash up the same night

Oil-based ink left overnight on rollers will cost you a morning.

Keep every spoiled sheet

They become endpapers, notebooks and the backing for the next job.

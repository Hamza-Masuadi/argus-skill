# Tern House

Build the website for Tern House, a small coastal hotel. You have complete creative freedom: develop the identity, typography, colour, layout, motion and copy yourself. Do not ask me to choose anything. It should feel like a real, well-loved independent hotel with an exceptionally good website, not a template.

## The business
- Tern House, a nine-room hotel with a restaurant on the harbour at St Mawes, Cornwall. A Georgian harbourmaster's house, reopened in 2019 by Isla and Ben Trevena. Open all year.
- Guests: couples and small groups from London, Bristol and the Midlands, 30 to 65, for two or three nights; walkers and sailors; locals for Sunday lunch. Most arrive from a recommendation, Instagram, or a search like "boutique hotel St Mawes".
- Why Tern House: every room faces the water; the restaurant cooks what the day boats land (the menu is written at 4pm); breakfast until 11; dogs welcome in four rooms; the ferry to Falmouth leaves from the door.
- Main action: check availability and book a room (dates, guests, room; no real payment here). Second: book a table.

## Rooms (invent detail in the same spirit)
- Harbour rooms (4), from £185 a night: king bed, window seat over the quay.
- Loft rooms (3), from £225: under the eaves, roll-top bath, the best view.
- Garden rooms (2), from £165: ground floor, own terrace, dog friendly.
Each needs a short description, size, what you see from the window, and what is included (breakfast always).

## Page
One page plus a working availability and booking flow (dates, guests, room, details, confirmation; no real payment, do not pretend one was taken) and a simple table request. It should include: an opening that puts you there, the rooms, the restaurant and today's menu, what to do from the door by season, getting there, and a strong ending. Many visitors are on a phone.

## Pictures
Use real stock photographs (and a short video loop if a good one exists) of a Cornish harbour, the sea, calm interiors, food and coast, chosen as one consistent, sharp, well-lit set. The business is invented: say so once in the footer.
